## The business

**KoraFlow turns "maybe" into confirmed players.** Friends organize casual football inside their WhatsApp group with one shared link: players tap "I'm in", pay their share to lock their spot, the cost per person updates live, and if someone drops out KoraFlow finds a replacement or re-plans the teams.

- **Users (free):** casual football players and organizers in Bahrain, mainly aged 18–35.
- **Paying customer:** football pitch owners, who pay a commission on prepaid, confirmed group bookings.
- **Main competitors:** Malaeb, Book&Go, FIELD, FC Pickup, FunPilot, and WhatsApp itself.
