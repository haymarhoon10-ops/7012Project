## 2. Requirements by stage (from the brief)

**Stage 1, Individual (each student submits a separate document)**
- [ ] Personality test summary (16Personalities)
- [ ] Key skills, interests, strengths (rubric "Excellent" also wants **weaknesses and team role**)
- [ ] Three ideas that match their sweet spot, with why each fits them
- [ ] At least two ideas proposed to the group, each with: problem, solution, target customer, value, why it is an opportunity
- [ ] Personality-test results and reflection go in the final ZIP

**Stage 2, Group: idea evaluation**
- [ ] All ideas (at least 6) in a weighted matrix, each criterion scored 1–10
- [ ] Suggested criteria: problem importance, demand, market size, uniqueness, technical feasibility, financial feasibility, resources and skills, competition, scalability, social/environmental value, team interest
- [ ] Total scores, and a written justification of the final choice (marks are for evaluation quality, not the top score)

**Stage 3, Group: customer and market validation**
- [ ] Clear target customer definition
- [ ] Customer persona
- [ ] Survey and interview questions (workshop asks for 8–10 of each)
- [ ] At least 10 valid survey responses per member (30 total)
- [ ] At least 3 short customer interviews per team
- [ ] Summary of findings, with charts
- [ ] Competitor analysis of at least 3 competitors or alternatives
- [ ] Explanation of changes made to the idea because of feedback
- [ ] Evidence in appendices (survey summaries, interview notes, screenshots, photos)

**Stage 4, Group: Business Model Canvas**
- [ ] All 9 blocks, consistent with the research and the financials

**Stage 5, Group: financial plan (in BHD)**
- [ ] Start-up costs
- [ ] Funding requirements and sources
- [ ] Sales forecast
- [ ] Operating expenses
- [ ] **Three-year** projected profit and loss (see issue A below)

**Stage 6, Group: MVP**
- [ ] MVP that shows the main value (clickable prototype, website, working app, etc.)
- [ ] Feedback from at least 5 potential users, and what was learned
- [ ] Evidence: screenshots, video, prototype files or a working link

**Stage 7: final pitch**
- [ ] About 10 minutes plus 5 minutes of questions; every member presents and answers
- [ ] Covers: problem, solution, target customers, validation evidence, competitive advantage, BMC highlights, revenue model, financial feasibility, MVP demo, future plans

## 4. Issues that need the lecturer's confirmation

- **A. One year vs three years.** The brief asks for a three-year P&L, but the template (Startup_One_Year_Projection.xlsx) covers only 12 months, Sep 2026 to Aug 2027. Default: complete the template exactly, then add a separate clearly labelled Year 2 and Year 3 summary.
- **B. Business plan template is missing.** The brief says the Word business plan must follow "the provided template", but no such template was uploaded.
- **C. Contribution declaration form is missing.** The brief requires a signed declaration; no form was uploaded. Default: we draft a simple one.
- **D. Three ideas or two?** Stage 1 asks each student for three sweet-spot ideas, and also says "at least two" go to the group. Default: each student lists 3 in their individual document and puts their best 2 forward, giving 6 for the group matrix.
- **E. Which evaluation criteria?** The brief's group matrix suggests 11 criteria scored 1–10. The Session 2 Part 1 activity uses a different, 21-criterion personal list with percentage weights. Default: brief's 11 criteria, using the activity's weighting method (weight x score). The "Idea Systemic Evaluation.xlsx" you mentioned was not uploaded; the method is clear from the .docx example.
- **F. ZIP name.** The example uses a 3-digit section ("001"). Ours could be `01_01_202303596` or `001_01_202303596`.
- **G. Rubric bands for the financial plan** have identical text in every column, so the bands can't be told apart. Probably a template error.
- **H. Deadline: resolved.** Final submission is 27 December 2026.
